\noindent A full resolution to Gap~2 of ideas\_v1 --- ``Location as
a sort,'' the open item that survived untouched through sixteen
staging versions while Gap~1 was closed. Worked out and verified
over several turns; recorded here as one connected entry rather than
scattered across the conversation that produced it.

\textbf{Blocking condition, stated before anything else in this
file: this resolution cannot be merged into Paper~1 on its own.} It
depends on the meta-vector taxonomy itself --- information vectors
versus meta vectors, as a formal distinction sitting on top of
Axiom~7 --- being imported first, as its own prior step. That
taxonomy is already destined for Paper~1 (ideas\_v11), but is not
yet merged there. Everything below is a specification of what goes
\emph{into} that taxonomy once it exists, not a thing that can be
written into the current paper by itself.

\section{Gap~2, restated from source}

ideas\_v1's own words, unedited: \emph{``Location as a sort --- a
third candidate. The existing paragraph names two candidates and
adopts neither. A third was tested here: dedicate separate, disjoint
dimensions to content, to time, and to location, rather than letting
any of them share an axis.''} Verified there: two shifts through
disjoint dimensions commute exactly; content stays recoverable after
both. Left open there, in its own words: \emph{``The encoding itself
--- what specific rotation a given time value or location corresponds
to --- is not stated anywhere. The geometry now supports it cleanly;
the mapping does not exist yet.''} That is the specific gap this
entry closes.

\section{The architectural decision: meta vector, not sort}

Tested against the paper's own existing distinction, not decided by
preference. The cap $r\le1$ encodes strength of \emph{content} ---
how activated a claim is. Location and time are not gradable claims;
they are coordinates describing where or when content applies.
Checked against the current paper directly: this is the same line
already drawn for Assembly's own seat, word for word --- \emph{``A
seat is external bookkeeping --- a place a unit occupies, carrying
no meaning and entering no chart. A location sort would be a unit in
its own right\ldots and would have to pass Axiom~0 on its own
terms.''} Meta vector is the seat-shaped answer; sort is the
full-unit-shaped one. Time has its own independent precedent
pointing the same way: the existing time-bucket metadata is already
described as \emph{``a fact about how a particular walk was
assembled, not a property its occupant carries into the next
one''} --- meta in spirit, already, before this entry names it so
formally.

The cost, named rather than hidden: a sort gets Mix, Bundle, and $T$
for free once admitted; a meta vector does not, and needs its own
grouping mechanism instead. That mechanism is already sketched,
separately, destined for Inference (ideas\_v11): querying several
atoms' meta vectors, confirming they sit close, promoting one shared
value up to the group.

\section{Location: one vector, verified}

Standard $(\theta,\phi)$ from latitude and longitude, the same
structure $\hat n$ already has, with zero adaptation needed: latitude
is bounded $[-90^\circ,90^\circ]$, matching $\theta$'s own bounded
range exactly; longitude is cyclic $[-180^\circ,180^\circ)$, matching
$\phi$ exactly. Verified directly on real coordinates --- London
$(51.5^\circ\text{N},0.1^\circ\text{W})\to(0.6225,-0.0011,0.7826)$,
Tokyo $(35.7^\circ\text{N},139.7^\circ\text{E})\to
(-0.6194,0.5252,0.5835)$, Sydney
$(33.9^\circ\text{S},151.2^\circ\text{E})\to
(-0.7273,0.3999,-0.5577)$ --- all unit norm to six decimal places,
all distinct. Ready as stated; no open question remains here.

\section{Time: the obvious encoding fails, verified precisely; the
fix does not}

\paragraph{The naive mapping (hour on $\theta$, minute on $\phi$)
was tested, not assumed, and broke in two distinct ways.} First, a
pole collapse: at hour$=0$, every minute value lands at the
identical point $(0,0,1)$, since $\theta=0$ is a pole and every
$\phi$ is degenerate there. Second, and worse: $11\!:\!59$ and
$0\!:\!00$, one minute apart, land at Euclidean distance
$\approx1.9\overline{9}$ --- antipodal, the maximum possible
separation on the sphere --- because $\theta$ was never built to
wrap, and sweeping hour across $[0,\pi]$ treats the end of the cycle
as the opposite pole from its start rather than its neighbor.

\paragraph{The fix stays inside existing machinery and was verified
to work.} Put minute on $\phi$ (already cyclic) and hour on $\chi$
(the atom's own existing phase coordinate, also already cyclic),
holding $\theta$ fixed at the equator so neither hand ever touches a
pole. Verified: $11\!:\!59$ and $0\!:\!00$ now land $6^\circ$ apart
in minute and under $1^\circ$ apart in hour, both hands fully
independent. $\theta$, freed from a job it was never suited for, sits
unused --- available for a third hand (seconds) if ever wanted, which
would make this a structurally exact match for a standard three-hand
analog clock: two coordinates already on the atom, one still spare.

\section{Timestamp: a register, not a scalar, scaled to $N$}

Not one timestamp per atom --- one per contributing vector. A simple
atom ($N=1$) carries one; a Collapsed atom carries $N$, since
different contributions can genuinely have been born at different
moments, and Collapse's whole design principle is keeping every
contribution rather than averaging it away. This costs nothing new
to keep synchronized: UnCollapse already removes exactly one
contribution, $N\to N-1$ (checked directly against the current
paper's own stated rule), so the same operation removes that
contribution's timestamp alongside it, automatically.

\section{Age: derived, never stored, and cannot correctly be
otherwise}

$\text{age}=\text{now}-\text{timestamp}$, computed on demand, the
same shape as $V_{\text{derived}}$ and Axiom~7's seven metrics ---
all read from stored data, none independently stored themselves.
This is not a convenience; a stored age would be wrong the instant
time passed, so deriving it is the only version that is ever
actually correct. ``Now'' itself is external context the formal
system assumes rather than defines --- the same status the paper
already gives lexicon standardization: \emph{``sociological and
empirical; it is not an axiom.''}

\section{Expiry: a second, genuinely new register --- not derivable,
unlike age}

Raised one turn after timestamp, under the same phrase (``time
period'') age had already used --- flagged and separated here so the
phrase does not carry two meanings going forward: \textbf{age} is
how old, derived; \textbf{expiry} is when a contribution stops being
valid, and unlike age, it cannot be computed from timestamp alone.
No universal rule maps birth-time to shelf-life --- different
contributions to one Collapsed atom plausibly expire at different
rates (three sensor readings --- humidity, pressure, windspeed ---
collapsed together need not go stale together). Expiry is therefore
genuinely new information, requiring its own register, same length
as the timestamp register, shrinking in lockstep with it under the
same $N\to N-1$ UnCollapse rule. It yields its own derived check, the
same shape as age: $\text{now}<\text{expiry}_i$, computed per vector,
never stored.

\paragraph{Left deliberately open.} Whether Mix or Collapse should
automatically skip expired contributions, or whether validity
filtering is a separate reading applied before either operator runs,
is a real design decision and is not answered here.

\section{Two caveats, kept honest rather than smoothed over}

Timestamps help disambiguate the degenerate UnCollapse case (two
contributions sharing identical $(r,\hat n,\chi)$) most of the time,
but are not a guaranteed substitute for the dedicated $id$ field
from ideas\_v9: two contributions ingested in the same batch could
share both content and birth time, and only a construction-guaranteed
$id$ --- not a timestamp --- would still separate them.

\section{Inventory, and where each piece actually lands}

Five meta-vector purposes now specified: $V_{\text{derived}}$ and
link vectors (already named, ideas\_v11), location, the timestamp
register, and the expiry register. Per ideas\_v11's own existing
split, confirmed unchanged by anything here: the taxonomy,
$V_{\text{derived}}$, location, timestamp, and expiry are all
\emph{defined} in Paper~1; link vectors are destined for Paper~2
(Assembly); the grouping mechanism that actually \emph{uses}
location and the time registers is destined for Inference. This
entry adds specification to the first category. It does not change
the other two.

\section{Status}

Mathematically complete and verified: the architectural decision,

Not mergeable into Paper~1 as a standalone edit, for the reason
