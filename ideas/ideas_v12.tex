\noindent v11 compared the paper against this staging document and
found gaps, stale symbols, and one naming collision. This version is
a different kind of check, requested separately: a full internal
audit of \texttt{PA\_ReWrite\_V15\_22.tex} against itself --- every
cross-reference individually verified, every use of the new verb
names checked for drift, and the document read for whether it still
reads as one cohesive statement now that Collapse carries full,
axiom-level standing. Everything found was fixable directly in the
paper, not a conceptual gap needing to be parked; the fixes are
recorded here for the historical trail, and the result is
\texttt{PA\_ReWrite\_V15\_23.tex}.

\section{Link audit: clean, one structural note}

Every label in the document (96) and every reference usage (198
individual call sites, not just unique labels) was checked --- not
only that each resolves, which the compiler already guarantees, but
that the target's own content actually matches the claim the
surrounding sentence makes about it. Zero mismatches found. Zero
orphaned references, zero duplicate labels.

One structural note, not a defect: \texttt{eq:lexicon\_cast} is used
in Section~3.1 (Bundle, path, result) before it is defined in
Section~6 (Several lexicons, one concept sphere) --- a genuine
forward reference, correctly resolving to the right equation, just
pointing ahead of where it is stated. Checked against the original
source: this forward reference predates the terminology rewrite and
the Section~2/3 restructuring entirely: this exact sentence, in this
exact relative position, forward-references the same concept in the
un-rewritten paper. Not introduced by anything done here; not a
broken link; recorded only so it is not mistaken for a side effect
of the restructuring later.

\section{Terminology sweep: two stragglers found and fixed}

Beyond what earlier passes already caught (the systematic Collapse
noun/verb fix, the Five-to-Six-verbs correction, the Table~2 column):

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One more ``a Collapse'' noun-phrase}, missed in the
earlier sweep, sitting in the Abstract itself: ``anti-phase
cancellation to black is that reading, not a Bundle or a Collapse.''
Same ambiguity as the six instances fixed before --- a reader could
read ``a Collapse'' as Axiom~7's structure rather than the verb.
Corrected to ``not what Bundle or Collapse would do,'' preserving
the parallel contrast without the noun-phrase risk.
\item \textbf{One retired verb still active}, in the Forest costume:
``$\uplus$ gathers them into one bowl.'' ``Gather'' was retired as
Bundle's name specifically to stop being used as the verb describing
this operator's own action; this instance described exactly that
action with exactly that retired verb. Corrected to ``$\uplus$
Bundles them.''
\end{itemize}

Confirmed zero remaining instances, checked directly: ``Gather''/
``gather'' (any case), ``MixMerge,'' the old $\oplus$/$\ominus$
assignment, any bare noun-phrase use of ``Collapse.'' The verb
inventory is now used consistently in every one of its roughly 50
occurrences across the document, not merely in the sections
originally rewritten.

\section{Cohesion: the document's own summaries of itself, checked
against what it now contains}

This is the one real category of finding --- not a conceptual gap,
but the document's own self-description trailing behind its own
content. Collapse has a full section, a proven non-idempotence
result, a column in the operator-legality table, and a place in the
verb-count headline. Three separate places where the paper
summarizes ``what this does,'' written or last touched before
Collapse reached that standing, still described only two ways of
combining units where the current paper has three:

\begin{itemize}
\item The Abstract's own pipeline sentence --- ``Bundle~$\to$~$T$~$\to$~result
is one pipeline; assemble~$\to$~rest is the other'' --- silently
omitted Collapse. Added: ``Collapse its count-preserving alternative
when every contribution must survive intact.''
\item The Conclusion's closing summary --- ``What was written is two
pipelines on the same unit'' --- described Bundle$\to T$ and Assembly
only. Added the same Collapse clause at the equivalent point.
\item The Scope-of-the-claim paragraph, a third occurrence of
materially the same two-way summary (``through a path... or by
letting external seats rest under assembly''), had the identical gap.
Fixed the same way, for consistency with the two above.
\end{itemize}

One further cohesion gap, different in kind: the section that
actually \emph{proves} UnMix's impossibility (``Rotational
transposition, and what division is not'') never once says the word
``UnMix'' in its own text --- it is reachable from UnMix's own
section by a forward reference, but carries nothing pointing back. A
reader arriving at the proof directly, rather than by reading
linearly from the verb table, would have no way to know this is the
same fact. Added the name and symbol directly into the proof
section's opening sentence.

Last, Future Work's own roundup of open items --- three named
paragraphs, each starting ``Open:'' --- did not include Collapse's
own already-stated open question (Collapse across differing sorts,
fully stated where Collapse itself is defined) even though the other
three open items from elsewhere in the paper are all gathered there.
Added a short fourth paragraph, pointing back to the full statement
rather than repeating it, so the roundup does not silently omit it.

\section{What this review did not find}

No new conceptual gap, beyond the three already filed in
Section~4 of v11 (Collapse across differing sorts, Collapse as $T$'s
actor, Assembly's silence on Collapse). This pass was looking for a
different failure mode --- the document disagreeing with itself,
not the document being silent on a question --- and on that
specific question, the answer is that it does not, once the five
fixes above are applied. No internal contradiction was found between
any two stated properties of Bundle, Remove, Collapse, UnCollapse,
Mix, or UnMix; no axiom's content was found to assume a Collapse
behavior inconsistent with the one actually proven; no section
written before the terminology rewrite was found using a retired
term to make an active claim, beyond the two instances in
Section~2 above.
