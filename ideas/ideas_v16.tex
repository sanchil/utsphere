\noindent Two documents, read together: an external-style review of
\texttt{Pattern\_Arithmetic\_v15\_21.tex} across simplicity,
consistency, and reproducibility, and a response proposing a
concrete two-paper split. Before parking either, the review's
checkable claims were verified directly against the current paper
rather than taken on faith --- every specific number, every claim
about where a term is or isn't used, checked and reported below.
Nothing here is adopted; this is the full record for a future
decision.

\section{The review, verified}

\paragraph{Every worked number checked, all exact matches.}
Sun-then-Wind $(-0.866,-0.500,0.011)$, Wind-then-Sun
$(0.278,-0.500,0.820)$, the $0.6\angle0^\circ$/$0.8\angle120^\circ$
Mix result $r'\approx0.721$ at $73.9^\circ$, the equator offset at
$120^\circ$, and the enclosure freeze step counts ($628$ at
$\delta=0.02$, $1261$ at $\delta=0.01$) all appear in the current
paper exactly as the review states them. Not approximately ---
checked character for character where the paper prints them.

\paragraph{``Axiom~7 reads as a second paper lodged inside the
first'' --- checked, and true without qualification.} Every
occurrence of $S_{\text{atom}}$, $\mathcal{V}_{\text{atom}}$,
$\gamma_{\text{atom}}$, $\mathcal{E}_{\text{atom}}$,
$\alpha_{\text{atom}}$, and $\sigma_{\text{atom}}$ in the current
paper --- $22$ occurrences total --- falls strictly inside Axiom~7's
own subsection. Not one appears in Bundle, Mix, $T$, or offset's own
treatment anywhere else in the document. The review's claim is not
an impression; it is a count.

\paragraph{``White is used twice'' --- checked, and the paper already
says so in its own words.} Axiom~3 states, as its own equation:
``White $=$ saturation of the bowl, or a full-spectrum name.'' Two
senses, acknowledged in the text itself, exactly as the review
reports --- not a hidden overload the review discovered, but one the
paper already names and moves past.

\paragraph{The ``checked directly'' precision claims --- present,
and genuinely without a reproducibility path.} The inversion-error
table ($1.1\times10^{-16}$ at several dimensions, $5.6\times10^{-17}$
at others), the UnMix residual of $10^{-17}$, and the
``verified to six decimal places'' line for the
$\mathcal{E}_{\text{atom}}$ identity are all present in the current
text, stated as results. No supplementary code, configuration file,
or appendix exists anywhere in the document or its outputs. A reader
has exactly what the review says: the printed number, and the
formula it came from, with the orientation convention for
higher-dimensional cases left to be reconstructed.

\paragraph{The technical consistency claims --- checked, all hold.}
$A\uplus A\uplus A\uplus A\setminus A=\emptyset$ appears verbatim.
Mix's non-associativity under the cap is stated directly: ``the cap
makes the binary operator non-associative.'' Both match the review's
description exactly.

\paragraph{What this verification adds.} Not a correction --- every
claim checked came back accurate. What it adds is that the review's
recommendations now rest on confirmed facts about the current paper,
not on an impression of an earlier or different one. The ``cut
Axiom~7'' recommendation in particular is now a verified count
($22$ uses, $0$ outside Axiom~7), not a stylistic judgment to weigh
against a competing stylistic judgment.

\section{The two-paper split, as proposed}

\paragraph{The cut.} Paper 1 (the typed algebra): the 4-tuple,
Axiom~0, Bundle and Remove, Mix and the refusal across directions,
Collapse and UnCollapse, $T$, offset, the sort table, and the UnMix
argument. Its sentence: a combination rule should match the unit, and
these verbs preserve different things. Paper 2 (assembly and the
enclosure): the assembly signature, the energy, seats, the freeze,
camps and bodies, and the printed six-unit replay. Its sentence: two
units can be related without being fused, and seats left under that
pull freeze into camps only at a stated threshold.

\paragraph{Where Axiom~7's metrics and the costumes go: neither
paper.} Collapse needs only the counted list of vectors ($N$ and the
vectors themselves), not the seven derived statistics. The enclosure
needs only $r$ and, optionally, a resultant weight. The storm,
forest, table-and-chair, and pathogen costumes restate $\uplus$
versus $T$ and are required for neither paper's own argument.

\paragraph{What must travel, specifically, or an idea breaks.} Four
items, stated as requirements rather than suggestions:
\begin{itemize}
\item Paper 2 needs roughly two pages of shared preamble from Paper 1
--- the 4-tuple, the fact that nothing in $(r,\hat n,\chi,\tau)$
says where a unit sits, and the repulsion-free bracket
$3+\hat n_A\cdot\hat n_B\in[2,4]$ --- cited, not rebuilt.
\item Paper 1 should state Assembly as a signature only: both units
survive, the new object is a contact and an energy that asks for a
seat. The full $U_{AB}$ formula, which depends on $d_{AB}$, belongs
in Paper 2, since the current text already says the verb is
incomplete until the enclosure exists; leaving a half-defined
$U_{AB}$ in Paper~1 is named as the one genuine risk to the split.
\item $\mathtt{chladni}$ stays in Paper~1. It is the evidence that
Mix across directions is refused, not a result about plates, and the
type table exists because of it.
\item The higher-dimensional form of $T$ stays in Paper~1, with
offset's plane transport alongside it. Neither depends on seats.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What the split costs, named directly rather than
minimized.} A reader of Paper~2 will not see why Mix is
non-associative; a reader of Paper~1 will not see a camp freeze.
This is called a loss of juxtaposition, not of either idea: each
example already stands on its own printed numbers (verified above)
without the other half present for rhetorical support.

\paragraph{The two wrong ways to make this cut, named explicitly as
a warning.} Splitting at Axiom~7's position in the document, or
moving Collapse to Paper~2 because it comes later in the text,
separates a verb from the unit it acts on --- a structural mistake,
not a simplification. Splitting off related work or the costumes is
a deletion, correctly identified as not the same operation as
writing a second paper.

\paragraph{The condition for the split to work.} Paper~1 ends by
naming the seat it refuses to supply; Paper~2 begins from that
refusal. The cut is by question, not by length.

\section{Where this connects to the metrics already parked
(ideas\_v13--v15)}

Not noted in either source document, since neither had the other's
context: the ``cut Axiom~7's metrics'' recommendation and the
mass/density/energy naming question already under discussion are the
same material viewed from two directions. ideas\_v13 asked which of
$S_{\text{atom}}$, $\mathcal{V}_{\text{atom}}$, $\gamma_{\text{atom}}$,
$\mathcal{E}_{\text{atom}}$ earn a physics name; this review asks
whether the whole catalogue earns a place in \emph{this} paper at
all, given that none of the four verbs actually reads from it. The
$D$/$N$ pair added to the Collapse section since (mass-number/
atomic-number) is unaffected either way: it already depends only on
the counted list of vectors, which --- per the split proposal
above --- is exactly what travels with Collapse regardless of where
the seven-metric catalogue ends up. If the split is ever adopted, the
open naming questions from ideas\_v13--v15 would need to be
re-homed wherever $\mathcal{E}_{\text{atom}}$ and its siblings
land, or shelved as belonging to neither paper's own argument, the
same way the costumes are described here.
