\section*{0 · The soul of the paper, on one page}
\begin{center}\fbox{\parbox{0.96\textwidth}{\small\textbf{The soul in eight paragraphs}\par\smallskip
\textbf{Thesis (§1).} Mathematics need not be performed on numbers alone. Wherever a domain supplies a \emph{unit of truth} — the smallest thing it treats as one — and a small set of rules by which such units combine, obeyed consistently, the unit and its rules form an \emph{arithmetic of that domain}. Counting ($1+1=2$, “how many?”) is one instance, not the only one: the word “add” names a different rule, and therefore a different kind of unit, for a colour, a note, a word.

\medskip
\textbf{The unit (Axiom 1, §2.2).} One typed point, not a field: $x=(r,\hat n,\chi,\tau)$ with strength $r\in[0,1]$, direction $\hat n\in S^2$ (a \emph{semantic} address, not a place), phase $\chi\in[0,2\pi)$, sort $\tau$ (which buttons may be pressed). The sphere is the stage; the unit is the vector.

\medskip
\textbf{The spine (§3.1).} A \emph{bowl} of seated units ($\uplus$) is read by a \emph{path} ($T$) to a \emph{result}; a result may be seated again (\emph{levels are closed}). A unit does not know what it constitutes: \textbf{a seated word does not know the poem; the path knows it.} One bowl, several ways to read it.

\medskip
\textbf{Six verbs, six questions.} $\uplus$ \emph{what is present?} · $\oplus$ \emph{what is combined, nothing discarded?} · $\boxplus$ \emph{what composite results?} · $T$ \emph{what happens, in what order?} · offset \emph{what walk is reused?} · $\bowtie$ \emph{how do two units relate while both still sit?} Three have a counterpart that takes something back ($\setminus$, $\ominus$); Mix's own, $\boxminus$, is proven impossible.

\medskip
\textbf{Six laws worth remembering.} $A\uplus A=A$ · $A\oplus A\neq A$ for every $A$ · Mix is lossy and, under the cap, non-associative · $T_aT_b\neq T_bT_a$ yet $T_{w^*}T_w=\mathrm{id}$ · Assembly never repels ($U_{AB}<0$) · UnMix does not exist.

\medskip
\textbf{What it refuses, on purpose.} Mix across differing directions (a claim about $\boxplus$ at one shared direction, not a bar: a two-stage operator is neither adopted nor barred) · UnMix · a geometric reading for any sort with no structure-preserving map (Axiom 0; most physical domains) · a relation across sorts (Collapse holds them side by side and claims none) · treating a costume (storm, forest, table and chair) as a model.

\medskip
\textbf{What it keeps honest.} Every law is deterministic: the same printed starting units — read at the same time, where any carries an expiry — give the same result. Every choice (the clip, $\alpha=\pi r$, $\eta$, $\delta$, the census cut, $\epsilon_{\text{allow}}$) is a \emph{named convention}, not a truth of the algebra.

\medskip
\textbf{How an atom is counted (§2.10).} An atom is $N$ \emph{cores} holding $Q$ informational \emph{vectors}; $D\le N$ of the cores are distinct. A vector is held by exactly one core, and which core holds which vector (the grouping) is part of the atom. Teaching atom: $N=Q=1$. Empty atom (black): $N=1$, $Q=0$. Beside its content an atom may carry \emph{meta vectors} (time, expiry, place, links, $V_{\text{derived}}$) that describe it and are no part of what it says (§2.11).}}\end{center}

\section*{4 · Metrics (Axiom 7, §2.10)}
All seven metrics are written $\mathcal{I}$ (script I, the information atom) with a subscript: str, vol, mean, en, agr, vcon, scon. Named in the paper: \emph{atom strength}, \emph{atom volume}, \emph{mean vector volume}, \emph{atom coherence}, \emph{effective vector count}; $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{en}}$ is the atom's \emph{information energy}; $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{agr}}$, $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{vcon}}$, $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{scon}}$ are its fifth, sixth and seventh metrics: \emph{atom agreement}, \emph{volume concentration} and \emph{shell concentration}. All presuppose an atom of $Q$ informational vectors $v_i=r_i\hat n_i$. None is used by any verb; each is read over the cores valid at the reading time. Identity: $\lvert V_{\text{derived}}\rvert^2=\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{en}}+2\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j(\hat n_i\!\cdot\!\hat n_j)$, the structure of a variance of a sum.

\section*{5 · Meta vectors (§2.11)}
\textbf{What they are.} Values that describe an atom and are no part of what it says. Not capped; not informational; counted in neither $N$ nor $Q$; outside the seven metrics and $D$; no admissibility of their own; \textbf{not a coordinate} (the four coordinates and the sort tag are unchanged). They ride with the cores they describe. Anything beyond that — what a meta vector \emph{means} — is extracted by context or by the path that reads it.

\subsection*{Rules that govern all of them}
{\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}\begin{longtable}{L{3.0cm}L{13.6cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{Statement} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\textbf{Form} & Always vectors, with no constraint on form. A scalar meta vector takes a designated axis of its core and carries its value as the coordinate there (uncapped), so vector distance is the difference of values; more coordinates (a plane or more) where a kind needs them. Which axis suits which kind is a recommendation (a catalogue, §6.5), not an axiom. \\
\textbf{Turning} & Turn an atom as a whole (same rotation and phase shift on each vector): $V_{\text{derived}}$ turns with it; scalar meta vectors are carried unchanged; angles, magnitudes and distances among the information vectors and $V_{\text{derived}}$ are preserved. \\
\textbf{Atoms are not edited} & An atom is a value. Change is Collapse or UnCollapse, each returning a new atom; recorded meta vectors never change after birth. There is no “last modified”. \\
\textbf{Equality} & Two tests. \emph{Equal}: the same vectors in the same grouping; meta vectors never enter (what $\uplus$, $D$ and UnCollapse use). \emph{The same individual}: equal, and the meta vectors agree too ($==$ against “is”). Equality is exact; measured readings are made comparable when they are seated. Were the timestamp part of the value, $N-D$ would always be $0$ and $A\uplus A=A$ would never apply. \\
\textbf{Identity and containment} & An atom is its vectors together with their grouping into cores (its structure; layout in storage is not the paper's concern); meta vectors are the labels. Identity is read from meta vectors (the identity key) and never enters equality; it can be dropped and UnCollapse still works by position. Containment is \emph{not} a meta vector: it is the grouping, part of the atom's value. \\
\textbf{Results} & Every result is a new atom with its own new timestamp and a backward link naming its operands; the cores that survive keep theirs. A result is also one new core on top of those: it carries its own $V_{\text{derived}}$ and may carry its own $V_{\text{location}}$ and $V_{\text{expiry}}$, supplied by a use; no verb computes them from the operands'. \\
\textbf{Expiry is a reading} & Everything is read at a time $t$ (default now). An expired core contributes no vectors and reads as a black core; $N$, $D$ are stored counts and do not change with $t$; $Q$, $V_{\text{derived}}$, the metrics and every process read only valid cores. An atom's expiry applies to all its cores; a contribution's expiry is carried onto each of its cores at Collapse. An expired core is noise: it and its vectors may be removed, by UnCollapse selected by expiry (a new atom; $N$ and $D$ then fall). A backward link is lineage, not participation, and stays readable. \\
\textbf{Nothing loses identity} & Under Collapse, Bundle and Mix the individual persists as meta vectors; this is persistence, not recovery. Mix leaves its sources unconsumed and names them by backward links; UnMix stays impossible. \\
\textbf{Absence is not zero} & For any meta vector: an absent location is not $(0,0)$; “expiry $0$ means none” would read as already expired. \\
\textbf{No verb changes one} & No verb changes a meta vector or takes one as an operand, except that $T$'s actor may be $V_{\text{derived}}$, read as it is; a verb may read one (Assembly reads $V_{\text{derived}}$ and the seat). \\
\textbf{Uses} & Group atoms for a task (collect, order, assemble). Grouping needs a comparison stated with each kind (great-circle for a location, cyclic for a clock face, equality for an identifier); none is common to all. Direction is the cleaner assembly rule; magnitude enters as a weight. Links declare structure and Assembly's energy produces it from geometry and a seat; the two are separate. Links can carry an order past $\uplus$, which does not read them, so order still comes from outside and the path is where it is walked. At contact the best partner of an atom of strength $0.60$ has strength $0.30$; at separation $3$ it is the strongest available. \\
