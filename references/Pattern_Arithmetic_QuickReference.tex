\section*{0. The Thesis (context, not a formal rule)}

The introduction now states the paper's governing idea directly, before any axiom:

\begin{quote}
\itshape Mathematics need not be performed on numbers alone. Wherever a domain supplies a unit of truth and a small set of rules by which such units combine, obeyed consistently, the unit and its rules together form an arithmetic of that domain.
\end{quote}

Color, musical notes, Chladni figures, valence chemistry, and the mitochondrion (joined without dissolving --- a natural-world instance of Assembly) are offered as \emph{evidence} that such arithmetics exist elsewhere, not as domains this paper models. Ice's six-fold growth is named as one such domain arithmetic in its own right --- and, by Axiom 0, exactly the kind refused a map onto this paper's sphere. This paper states its own rules as axioms rather than discovering them; the ``settle toward stability'' half of the thesis appears only in miniature, in the Enclosure (\S9).

\textbf{0} & Admissibility of Sorts & Before any operator can be used, a sort $\tau$ must earn the right to a geometric reading --- the table of who-may-use-what operators comes \emph{after} this, not instead of it. \\
\textbf{1} & The Normalized Unit Truth State ($r\le1$) & A pattern is one typed point, not a field: $x=(r,\hat n,\chi,\tau)$, $r\in[0,1]$, $\hat n\in S^2$, $\chi\in[0,2\pi)$. A bowl is a separate finite set of such atoms. \\
\textbf{2} & The Absolute Null State ($r=0$, Black) & Vanishing strength means direction and phase are unused --- an empty seat, not a field that happens to be zero everywhere. \\
\textbf{3} & Saturation (White) & The upper pole of the same bounded stage, not an infinite norm. Reached either as a single full-spectrum identity or as a bowl with many directions filled --- neither pushes any single arrow past $r=1$. \\
\textbf{4} & Gather and Remove ($\oplus$, $\ominus$) & $\oplus$ is set union on bowls --- it collects, it does not blend: $A\oplus A=A$. $\ominus$ removes one member. Set union needs no dimension, so this is unaffected by anything below. \\
\textbf{5} & Mix as a Reading ($\boxplus$) & A phasor operator: combines $\chi$ among states that already share a direction $\hat n$. Reads a bowl into one blended state; never moves an arrow onto a new axis. Lossy and non-associative under the cap --- see \S3 below. Dimension-blind wherever it applies, since it never leaves the shared axis. \\
\emph{(unnumbered)} & Rotation preserves the unit & $\mathbf{R}\in\mathrm{SO}(3)$ turns $\hat n$, leaves $r,\chi,\tau$ untouched. The geometric engine $T$ is built from, not a second kind of blend. \\
\textbf{6} & Assembly ($\bowtie$) & The fourth verb: given two intact units, how strongly do they belong together and where do they touch? $A\bowtie B=(A,B,\kappa_{AB},U_{AB})$ --- both units survive unchanged; nothing is fused. Never repels in any dimension: the alignment bracket $3+\hat n_A\cdot\hat n_B$ stays in $[2,4]$ regardless of $N_{\mathrm{dim}}$. \\
\textbf{7} & Informational Vectors \& Derived Per-Atom Metrics & An atom may be a \emph{bundle} of $N$ informational vectors rather than one --- still teaching atoms under one closure, not a new species of unit. Defines $V_{\text{derived}}$ and the seven scalar metrics of \S3. \\
\end{longtable}

\textbf{Two further operators are built on these axioms but are not themselves axioms} --- both now generalize beyond three dimensions, verified rather than merely claimed:
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{2pt}
\item \textbf{Transform ($T$)} --- \S3.4: an actor $w$ rotates an occupant $x$ about $w$'s own axis by $\alpha(r_w)=\pi r_w$. Beyond $N_{\mathrm{dim}}=3$, the actor's bundle supplies $N_{\mathrm{dim}}-2$ mutually orthogonal arrows to fix the axis (see the ladder in \S3 below); checked invertible and non-commutative up to $N_{\mathrm{dim}}=50$, exact at $N_{\mathrm{dim}}=3$.
\item \textbf{Offset} --- \S4.1: plants a new seat from three seats via log/exp/transport. The transport is now written for general dimension too: a rotation within the plane the two seats themselves span, needing no bundle, exact at $N_{\mathrm{dim}}=3$ against the original cross-product form.
\end{itemize}

$V_{\text{derived}}$ & $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^N v_i$ & The raw, \textbf{uncapped} vector sum of the bundle. Reads as an effective $(r,\hat n)$ pair when a single vector is needed --- but is \emph{not itself a unit} and needs a cast before acting as $T$'s actor $w$. & any $N\ge1$ \\

$\sigma_{\text{atom}}$ & $\dfrac{N^2}{N-1}\operatorname{Var}(s_1,\ldots,s_N)$, \newline $s_i=r_i^3/\mathcal{V}_{\text{atom}}$ & \textbf{Volume concentration.} $0$ when spread evenly, $1$ when one vector holds it all, at every $N$. & $N\ge2$ ($:=0$ at $N=1$) \\
$\sigma_{\text{shell}}(w)$ & $\dfrac{K^2}{K-1}\operatorname{Var}(p_1,\ldots,p_K)$, \newline bins of width $w$ (default $0.1$) & \textbf{Shell concentration.} Catches a blind spot $\sigma_{\text{atom}}$ has by construction: many vectors independently landing at the same magnitude. & $\ge2$ occ.\ shells ($=1$ at $K=1$) \\

$U_{AB}$ & $-\dfrac{r_Ar_B}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3+\hat n_A\cdot\hat n_B}{(d_{AB}^2+\varepsilon_{AB}^2)^{3/2}}$, \newline $\varepsilon_{AB}=r_A+r_B$ & Axiom 6 & Assembly's interaction energy. Always $<0$ for nonzero strengths --- never repels, in any dimension. \\

$z_B$, $\boxplus(B)$ & $\sum_{x\in B} r_xe^{i\chi_x}$; result $=(\min(1,\lvert z_B\rvert),\hat n,\arg z_B,\tau)$ & Axiom 5 & Mix's one-step bowl reading --- the reason binary Mix is non-associative under the cap. \\
$v$, transport, $\hat n_*$ & $v=\log_{\hat n_m}(\hat n_k)$; \newline $\hat n_*=\exp_{\hat n_w}(\text{transport}_{m\to w}(v))$ & \S4.1 & The copied walk: log the source pair, transport the tangent vector, exponentiate at the new seat. At $N_{\mathrm{dim}}=3$, transport is the original cross-product rotation about $\hat n_m\times\hat n_w$; in general, it is a rotation within the plane $\hat n_m,\hat n_w$ themselves span --- the two seats name the turning plane, so no bundle is needed. The two forms agree exactly at $N_{\mathrm{dim}}=3$. \\
$F_i$ & $-\nabla_{\vec P_i}\sum_{j\ne i}U_{ij}$ & \S9 & Force on a seated unit --- descended without mass or momentum. \\
freeze condition & $\max_i\lvert F_i\rvert<\eta$ & \S9.2 & A configuration below threshold $\eta$ is a \emph{checkpoint}: it may be frozen and taken as the accepted solution --- not exact rest, since the pull never reaches zero at finite distance, so left longer every configuration drifts toward one pile. \\
$\vec P_{c,k}$ & $\dfrac{\sum_{i\in G_k} r_i\vec P_i}{\sum_{i\in G_k} r_i}$ & \S9.3 & A camp's body --- the strength-weighted centre of its members, read \emph{after} motion, not sought during it. \\
$\epsilon_{\text{allow}}$ & (named, not derived) & \S9.3 & \textbf{Allowance.} Two checkpoints whose bodies differ by less than $\epsilon_{\text{allow}}$ are read as the same freeze; a fourth named convention alongside $\eta$, $\delta$, and the census cut --- not a truth of $U_{AB}$. A camp read this way is still only a checkpoint on its members, never a new unit in its own right. \\
\end{longtable}
